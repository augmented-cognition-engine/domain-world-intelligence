## Intelligence boundary

Visibility is not corroboration. The five new records do not enter the policy detector and do not
support the existing Reality Brief. The Brief remains grounded in the exact Federal Register and
White House closure. Wave 1 therefore proves a broader current picture without manufacturing five
new Signals from five new headlines.

## Reproduction

From this World checkout, with a current Core checkout:

```bash
/path/to/ace-core/.venv/bin/python \
  -m scripts.launch_atrium_ai_command_center \
  --core /path/to/ace-core
```

The page reports seven admitted sources, one active watch, one material Case, and 43 cited records.
The replay remains loopback-only and non-authorizing.
