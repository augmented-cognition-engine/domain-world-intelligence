# LinkedIn draft — World Intelligence 0.7.0-rc1

Two publishers do not necessarily equal two independent sources.

That sounds obvious. Most AI research and news-summary systems still flatten the distinction.

Today I have a concrete, reproducible developer-preview demo of a different approach: ACE World
Intelligence models the derivation chain before it writes the narrative.

In the frozen scenario, Coastal Wire and Harborview are different publishers—but both derive from
the same Ledger report. ACE correctly treats them as one evidence family. A separate Basin gauge
provides the second independent root required for corroboration.

Then a correction arrives.

ACE does not silently rewrite the prior Brief. It appends a governed impact projection showing the
exact dependency boundary:

- 11 downstream resources affected
- 9 Brief claims affected
- 6 direct and 5 transitive dependencies
- 16 resources explicitly unaffected
- maximum depth of 3

The original governed Brief keeps the same identity and replays byte-for-byte under its original
cutoff. Impact means dependency, not falsehood—the system shows what relied on the corrected record,
not what you are required to believe.

This is the direction for ACE:

entity graph → signal → shift → Brief → governed feedback

One reasoning and intelligence kernel. Domain behavior supplied by declarative packs. World
Intelligence is the first public proof that the same architecture can extend beyond marketing.

Important boundary: this release candidate uses a synthetic, hermetic, PREPARED scenario. It is not
a live news feed, not production-ready, and not an autonomous publisher. The next step is a governed,
read-only official-source adapter.

But the hard conceptual proof is now executable—not a slide.
